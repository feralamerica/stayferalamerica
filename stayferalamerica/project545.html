The Project 545 Tracker is an index of public records — and independent journalism about them. It maps, in detail, a coordinated foreign influence campaign as documented in Foreign Agents Registration Act (FARA) filings and other sworn government records. The premise is simple and strict: every claim links to a document. The tracker explicitly avoids speculation beyond what the filings demonstrate.

What it covers

  • Eight registered foreign agents involved in the campaign
  • Five distinct money routes totaling approximately $145 million
  • Detailed FARA filings and sworn documents, annotated
  • Specific operations — including work with political operatives and church-outreach efforts

How to read it

  • An interactive pipeline visualization of how the money moves
  • A timeline of filings and transactions
  • A cast of the named individuals
  • Primary-source documents with annotations
  • A series of investigative articles and an update log tracking new filings
Methodology: The tracker prioritizes transparency through direct document linking, detailed methodology disclosure, and a formal corrections policy. If you can add a filing, a correction, or a missing thread, send it here.
