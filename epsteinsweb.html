Epstein's Web maps the documented associations between named individuals, institutions, banks, and universities that appear in publicly released Epstein-related materials. It distinguishes between different kinds of documentation — associates, business contacts, witnesses, and staff — and deliberately excludes victims and survivors from the database.

Appearing here is not evidence of wrongdoing. Every entry notes how a person is documented — correspondence, travel records, deposition testimony, or a passing reference — with direct links to the original source. Red marks an accusation of involvement (with denials noted); other colors mark documented contact without implying wrongdoing.

The five source collections

  • Flight logs
  • Epstein's contact directory (the "black book")
  • Court records
  • 2025 emails from House Oversight and DOJ disclosures
  • Financial documents

How to explore it

  • Network visualization — connections drawn across all five document collections
  • Word cloud — names sized by how prominently they appear in the released materials
  • Searchable rolodex — individual profiles, each with its source links
  • Sortable list view — built for journalists and for accessibility
