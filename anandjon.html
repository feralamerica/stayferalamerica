The Anand Jon Record is a public-interest reference documenting the crimes, convictions, and release campaign surrounding fashion designer Anand Jon Alexander. It presents sourced evidence drawn from court filings and government records, and tracks not only what was proven in court but the organized effort now seeking to undo it.

The crimes & convictions

  • A 2008 California conviction on 16 felony counts, carrying a 59-years-to-life sentence
  • Guilty pleas in New York and indictments in Texas
  • A documented pattern of targeting aspiring models with false promises

The record

  • Court Records — case numbers and locations for public records across three states
  • Press Coverage — links to reporting from major outlets on the case
  • The Network — the people and organizations actively seeking his release, including family, nonprofits, and a youth-facing website
  • The Alliances — how entities such as the International Justice Alliance, the American Justice Alliance, and thebfly.co interconnect through shared leadership
Why it exists: The record tracks the documented crimes and convictions alongside the documented network working for his release — so the public can see both, sourced. If you have court filings, records, or information about the release campaign, send them here.
